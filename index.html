Hello, World!

Die Software is an independent game studio from Australia with a singular goal: making games driven by story and atmosphere, worlds you can get lost in and stories that stay with you long after you put the controller down. Games where quality comes first and community actually matters, a place where everyone has a seat at the table. Are you ready to roll with us?

03 Behind the die Draft copy

One developer. No face, just the games.

Die Software is a one-person studio. Design, programming, art and sound all come from a single desk, and the person rolling the dice would rather you knew the games than the name. This page follows the studio as it grows, from the first game to the ones after it.
